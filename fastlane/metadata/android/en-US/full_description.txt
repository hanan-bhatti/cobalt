Cobalt is an offline-first personal archive and capture app for Android. It stores links, notes, images, audio clips, code snippets, and movie, TV, anime and manga lists locally without requiring a Cobalt account. Local note storage supports shared history, recoverable drafts and saved Undo/Redo.

Capture Methods:
- Android Share Target: Save text, URLs, images, and videos directly from other apps via the system share sheet.
- Floating Overlay: Access a screen-edge panel to write quick notes, capture links, or trigger OCR without leaving your current app.
- Screen Region OCR: Capture a screenshot or crop a region on screen using MediaProjection to extract text and links.
- Quick Settings Tile: Launch OCR capture directly from the system Quick Settings panel.
- Voice Notes: Record audio memos with real-time waveform display, stored locally on device.
- Home Screen Widgets: View recent captures or trigger capture actions from your home screen.

Organization & Viewing:
- Supported Item Types: Links (with metadata previews), Markdown text notes, Photos/Images, Video clips, Code snippets, Audio recordings, and Movies/Anime.
- Folders & Archive: Organize items into custom folders or archive them to remove them from the main feed while keeping them stored.
- Search & Filters: Search across titles, content, extracted OCR text, and URLs, with filter chips for each media type.
- Media Hub: Track movies, TV, anime and manga with local ratings, watch/read statuses, bookmarks and cached episode details. Configure TMDB, Trakt and custom Jikan v4 endpoints in Media & Providers.
- Notes & Storage: Continuous Markdown editing, tables, search/replace, local font controls, recoverable drafts, saved versions, Trash and reviewed cleanup that protects unsaved writing.
- Sharing: Styled note, link, image and movie cards with smaller text, readable excerpts and continuation markers, image/Markdown export and bounded clipboard copy.
- Local Organization: Smart folder ranking, fuzzy search, capture activity and adaptive review reminders.

Network & Privacy Disclosures:
Cobalt stores all data locally on your device. It does not include analytics, tracking SDKs, or Cobalt-owned cloud synchronization. Certain optional features connect to external APIs:
- AI Screen OCR & Transcription: Sends selected image regions or voice notes to Google's Gemini API for processing. This requires you to provide your own Gemini API key in Settings.
- Movie & Anime Data: Queries The Movie Database (TMDb) and Jikan (MyAnimeList) for media details and poster images. TMDb searches require your own TMDb API key.
- Trailers: Embedded playback of YouTube trailers on media detail screens.

- Trakt: Optional user-authorized watch tracking and synchronization.
- Updates: GitHub release checks are opt-in in Release & Updates.
- Links and remote media: Metadata and images can contact saved URL hosts.
- Feedback: Choose to export/share a bug report or open a pre-filled GitHub/email submission. The optional experience survey uses 1–10 sliders and opens your email app with plain-text answers; you decide whether to send them.

Biometric locking, encrypted notes, a note-editing web companion and complete app backup/restore are not currently provided.

Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Uninstalling Cobalt can permanently remove local data.
